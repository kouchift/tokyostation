## Quick Facts

**Tokyo Station** is one of Japan's busiest railway stations, serving millions of passengers annually. Located in the Chiyoda ward of Tokyo, it's a major transportation hub connecting:
- Shinkansen (bullet trains)
- JR local and express trains
- Subway lines
- Bus terminals

## Getting Around

### Main Entrances
- Marunouchi Side (West)
- Yaesu Side (East)
- Nihonbashi Side (North)
- Southern Exit

### Key Platforms
- Shinkansen: Platforms 1-4
- Tokaido/Sanyo Lines: Platforms 14-17
- Chuo/Sobu Lines: Platforms 1-3 (B1F)
