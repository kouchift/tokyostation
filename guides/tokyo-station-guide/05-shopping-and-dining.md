# Shopping and Dining

## Shopping Areas

### Ground Floor & Upper Levels

**Gransta** (Main shopping complex)
- Multiple floors of retail
- Department stores
- International brands
- Open: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM (varies by store)

### Character Street
- Anime and manga merchandise
- Japanese pop culture items
- Tourist souvenirs
- Location: B1F
- Hours: 8:15 AM - 10:00 PM

### Duty Free Shops
- Tax-free shopping for international visitors
- Perfume, cosmetics, electronics
- Locations: Multiple throughout station
- Requires passport and flight information

### Department Store Food Halls
- Japanese and international cuisine products
- Bento boxes, snacks, beverages
- High quality prepared foods
- Great for travel provisions

## Dining Options

### Restaurants

**Ramen Yokocho** (Ramen Alley)
- Located: B1F
- Multiple ramen shops
- Traditional and modern styles
- Budget-friendly
- Hours: 10:00 AM - 11:00 PM

**Japanese Restaurants**
- Sushi, tonkatsu, tempura, udon
- Multiple options at various price points
- Authentic Japanese cuisine
- Takeout available

**International Cuisine**
- Italian, French, Korean, Chinese
- Fast casual and fine dining
- Located on multiple floors

### Cafes & Quick Bites
- Coffee shops with seating areas
- Bakeries with fresh pastries
- Convenience stores (24/7)
- Vending machines throughout

## Dining Price Ranges

| Type | Price Range | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Convenience store | ¥300-1,000 | Onigiri, bento, ramen |
| Casual restaurant | ¥1,000-3,000 | Ramen, udon, curry |
| Casual dining | ¥2,000-5,000 | Izakaya, casual Japanese |
| Restaurant | ¥5,000+ | Nicer restaurants, fine dining |

## Specialty Shops

- **BENTO**: Traditional Japanese lunch boxes
- **SOUVENIR**: Regional specialties from across Japan
- **TRAIN GOODS**: Railway-themed merchandise
- **BOOKSTORE**: Books, magazines, English available

## Payment Methods

- ✅ Cash (most reliable)
- ✅ IC Cards (Suica, Pasmo)
- ✅ Credit Cards (at larger stores)
- ✅ Mobile Payment (PayPay, Line Pay, etc.)

## Eating Etiquette Tips

1. **Slurping noodles** is acceptable and encouraged
2. **Finishing your meal** shows appreciation
3. **Tipping** is not expected or customary
4. **Chopsticks etiquette**: Don't stick upright in rice
5. **Photography**: Ask before photographing food or people
