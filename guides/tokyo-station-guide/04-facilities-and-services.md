# Facilities and Services

## Essential Facilities

### Restrooms (化粧室)
- **Locations**: Multiple throughout the station
- **Hours**: 5:00 AM - 12:00 AM (main floors)
- **Features**: Clean, modern facilities
- **Accessibility**: Wheelchair accessible restrooms available
- **Amenities**: Hand dryers, mirrors, changing tables

### Luggage Storage (荷物預かり)
- **Types**: Lockers and baggage delivery services
- **Locker Sizes**: Small (¥400-600/day), Large (¥700-1000/day)
- **Baggage Delivery**: Same-day and next-day delivery available
- **Hours**: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM (varies by location)

### Information Services

| Service | Location | Hours |
|---------|----------|-------|
| JR East Travel Centers | Multiple | 8:15 AM - 7:00 PM |
| Tourist Information | Yaesu Exit | 8:15 AM - 7:00 PM |
| Multi-language Support | Info Desks | Throughout day |

### Medical Services
- **First Aid**: Available at multiple locations
- **Pharmacies**: Located in shopping areas
- **Clinics**: On-site medical clinics for emergencies
- **24-Hour Services**: Limited emergency services available

## Transportation Services

### Ticket Offices
- **Locations**: Main concourse, Shinkansen area
- **Services**: Reservations, passes, discount tickets
- **Languages**: English and other languages available
- **Hours**: 8:15 AM - 7:00 PM (varies)

### IC Card Services
- **Suica/Pasmo Sales**: At ticket offices
- **Recharge**: Automatic machines throughout
- **Usage**: Train, bus, shopping, vending machines

### Money Exchange
- **Authorized Exchanges**: Located in shopping areas
- **Currency**: Major international currencies accepted
- **Hours**: Typical business hours

## Communication Services

### Phone & Internet
- **Free WiFi**: Available throughout the station
- **Mobile Charging**: Charging stations on multiple floors
- **SIM Cards**: Available for purchase
- **Phone Rental**: Tourist rental services available

## Accessibility Services

- **Wheelchair**: Available for loan (request at info desks)
- **Elevators**: Multiple throughout the station
- **Escalators**: Stairs and escalators in all areas
- **Accessible Parking**: Available near station

## Left Luggage & Parcel Services
- **Post Office**: Available for mailing packages
- **Courier Services**: Yamato, Sagawa, Japan Post
- **Next-Day Delivery**: Available to most locations in Japan
- **International Shipping**: Available
