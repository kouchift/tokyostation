# Emergency Information

## Emergency Services

### Police (警察)
- **Emergency Number**: 110
- **Non-Emergency**: Contact station police box (koban)
- **Multilingual Support**: Available for serious cases

### Fire/Ambulance (救急車)
- **Emergency Number**: 119
- **Response Time**: Very fast (typically minutes)
- **Treatment**: Excellent medical standards

### Station Staff
- **Always on Duty**: At ticket offices and information desks
- **First Aid**: Available throughout the station
- **Security Personnel**: Visible at major exits and platforms

## Health & Medical

### On-Site Medical Facilities
- **First Aid Stations**: Multiple locations
- **Medical Clinics**: Available for consultations
- **Pharmacies**: Drug stores with English-speaking staff
- **Hospitals**: Reference desk can direct you to nearest hospital

### For Visitors
- **English-Speaking Doctors**: Request at info desks
- **International Insurance**: Accepted at major hospitals
- **Medications**: Pharmacy services available

## Safety & Security

### General Safety
- Tokyo is very safe, but be alert for pickpocketing in crowded areas
- Avoid unattended belongings
- Keep valuables close and secure
- Don't accept drinks from strangers

### Crowded Periods
- Rush hours: 7-9 AM, 5-7 PM weekdays
- Weekends can be very crowded
- Station is well-managed with security and staff
- Emergency exits clearly marked

### What to Do If Lost
1. Approach any **blue-uniformed staff** member
2. Ask to go to nearest **Information Desk**
3. Station maps available at desks
4. Free WiFi for map access

## Scams & Precautions

### Be Aware Of
- Unlicensed taxis outside station
- Overpriced tourist services
- Lost passport/wallet scams
- Fake official websites

### Prevention
- Use official ticket offices only
- Verify prices before purchasing
- Keep copies of important documents
- Register with your embassy if staying long-term

## Lost & Found

### Items Left Behind
- **Location**: Central Lost & Found (inquire at info desk)
- **Process**: Describe item and location left
- **Timeframe**: Items held for approximately 30 days
- **Language**: English-speaking staff available

### Important Documents
- **Passport**: Immediately contact your embassy
- **Credit Cards**: Call issuing bank immediately
- **Tickets**: Go to ticket office of relevant rail company
- **Baggage**: Report to luggage services office

## Accessible Facilities for Emergency

- **Wheelchair Access**: Throughout station
- **Accessible Restrooms**: Multiple locations
- **Elevators**: All major areas covered
- **Staff Assistance**: Always available upon request

## Natural Disasters (Earthquakes, Typhoons)

### During Earthquake
- Station is well-designed for seismic activity
- Follow staff instructions
- Move away from electronic signs/panels
- Remain calm and stay in station if safer

### Information Access
- **Announcements**: Made in Japanese and English
- **TV Monitors**: Display emergency information
- **Staff**: Available to provide guidance
- **Phones**: Station phones available for use

## Before Your Trip

- **Travel Insurance**: Recommended for international visitors
- **Embassy Info**: Keep embassy contact with you
- **Medication**: Bring prescription documentation
- **Vaccination**: Check requirements for Japan
- **Travel Registration**: Consider registering with your embassy
