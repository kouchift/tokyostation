# Transportation Guide

## Rail Services at Tokyo Station

### Shinkansen (Bullet Train)
**Platform**: 1F (Shinkansen platform)

| Line | Destinations | Travel Time |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Tokaido | Kyoto, Osaka, Hiroshima | 2h 15min - 6h+ |
| Sanyo | Okayama, Hiroshima, Hakata | 3h 45min - 8h+ |
| Tohoku | Sendai, Aomori, Hakodate | 1h 30min - 3h+ |
| Akita | Akita | 3h 40min |
| Yamagata | Yamagata | 2h 28min |

**Ticket Purchase**:
- JR East Travel Service Centers
- Automatic ticket machines
- Online (Ekinet) for advance booking

### JR Local & Express Trains

**Main Lines**:
- **Chuo Line**: Central Tokyo, Shinjuku area
- **Sobu Line**: Chiba, Ryogoku
- **Keihin-Tohoku Line**: Shibuya, Yokohama
- **Tokaido Main Line**: Yokohama, Kamakura

**Platform Assignment**: Platforms 1-17 (signs indicate which trains)

### Subway Connections

| Marunouchi | Red | Shinjuku, Ikebukuro | B1F |
| Chiyoda | Green | Ginza, Otemachi | B1F |

## Buses & Highway Coaches

**Locations**: 
- Yaesu Side (East side) - Main bus terminal
- Multiple stops around the station

**Popular Routes**:
- Narita Express (N'EX): To Narita Airport
- Airport Limousine: To Haneda Airport
- Highway coaches to Nagoya, Osaka, etc.

## Taxi Services

- **Taxi Stands**: Multiple locations at each exit
- **Night Service**: Available 24/7
- **Payment**: Cash, IC card, credit card
- **Tip**: Not expected in Japan

## Ride-Sharing Options

- Uber, Waze, and other ride-sharing apps available
- Premium taxi services available
- Pick-up points designated near station exits

## Getting to Major Locations

### Narita Airport (60-90 minutes)
- Narita Express (N'EX) direct train
- Keisei Skyliner from nearby station
- Airport bus

### Haneda Airport (30-45 minutes)
- Keikyu Railway or Monorail from nearby
- Airport Limousine bus
- Ride-sharing

### Shibuya/Shinjuku (15-20 minutes)
- Yamanote Line or Central Line (nearby stations)
- Taxi or ride-sharing
