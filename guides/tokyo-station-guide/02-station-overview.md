# Tokyo Station Overview

## Station Statistics

**Tokyo Station** (Tōkyō-eki / 東京駅)
- **Opened**: December 20, 1914
- **Daily Passengers**: ~3.7 million
- **Annual Passengers**: ~1.3 billion
- **Rail Lines**: 5+ major lines
- **Platforms**: 30+
- **Floors**: 9 (including underground)

## Station Districts

### Shinkansen Area (Shinkansen Platform)
- Tokaido/Sanyo/Tohoku Shinkansen trains
- Modern, efficient infrastructure
- Direct connection to Narita Express

### JR Main Line (Platforms 14-17)
- Tokaido Main Line
- Chuo Line connections
- Other JR Express services

### Subway Lines
- **Marunouchi Line**: Central Tokyo connections
- **Chiyoda Line**: Central and Eastern Tokyo

### Yaesu Side (East Side)
- Shopping centers
- Restaurants
- Express buses to airports
- Hotels

### Marunouchi Side (West Side)
- Business district
- Hotels and offices
- Connection to Imperial Palace area

## Key Landmarks

1. **Tokyo Station Building (Historic)**: Restored historic facade
2. **Character Street**: Shopping area with anime/manga goods
3. **Ramen Yokocho**: Ramen restaurants (B1F)
4. **Tokyo Station Hotel**: Connected to the station

## Architecture Features

- Classic Baroque-influenced design (restored in 2012)
- Modern extension completed in phases
- Integrated mall (Gransta)
- Multi-level shopping and dining

## Accessibility

- Elevators and escalators throughout
- Wheelchair accessible routes
- Accessible restrooms
- Information desks with multilingual staff
