# Getting Started at Tokyo Station

## Welcome to Tokyo Station! 🚉

Tokyo Station (東京駅) is one of the most important transportation hubs in Japan. This guide will help you navigate the station with ease.

## How to Access Tokyo Station

### By Public Transport
- **Trains**: Tokyo Station is served by multiple railway companies including JR East, Tokaido Shinkansen, and Tokyo Metro
- **Subways**: Chiyoda Line and Marunouchi Line stations
- **Buses**: Multiple bus terminals available

### By Car
- **Taxi**: Taxi stands available at multiple exits
- **Ride-sharing**: Available through major app services
- **Parking**: Limited parking available (check info desks for details)

## Main Entrances Overview

| Entrance | Name | Best For |
|----------|------|----------|
| West | Marunouchi Exit | Hotels, Imperial Palace Area |
| East | Yaesu Exit | Shopping, Restaurants |
| North | Nihonbashi Exit | Central Tokyo |
| South | Southern Exit | Waterfront Areas |

## First-Time Visitor Tips

1. **Get Oriented**: Pick up a station map at the Information Desk
2. **IC Card**: Consider getting a Suica/Pasmo card for easy transit
3. **Language**: Most signs have English translations
4. **Time Management**: Allow extra time during rush hours (7-9 AM, 5-7 PM)
5. **Ask for Help**: Station staff speak various languages

## Station Layout

Tokyo Station has multiple levels:
- **B2F-B1F**: Subway platforms and underground shopping
- **1F**: Main concourse and exits
- **2F-4F**: Shopping, restaurants, and Shinkansen platforms

## Where to Get Help

- **Information Desks**: Multiple locations (see station map)
- **Staff**: Available at ticket counters and platforms
- **Mobile App**: Download Tokyo Station app for real-time information
- **Internet**: Free WiFi available throughout the station
